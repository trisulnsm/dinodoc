The full index of counter groups is in the [Counter Groups Reference](/docs/guide/counter-groups/). It lists every built-in group and every group added by Trisul Apps, with its layer, what it measures, its key format and GUID.

Each group has its own page with exact key formats, special keys such as `SYS:GROUP_TOTALS`, and the full meter table, checked against the source code.

Useful sections of the reference:

- [Key formats](/docs/guide/counter-groups/#key-formats): how IP addresses, ports, MACs and interfaces are stored as keys.
- [Special keys](/docs/guide/counter-groups/#special-key-sysgroup_total): `SYS:GROUP_TOTALS` and when it double counts.
- [Meter types](/docs/guide/counter-groups/#meter-types): rate counters, gauges, averages.
- [Built-in groups](/docs/guide/counter-groups/#common-groups) and [Trisul Apps groups](/docs/guide/counter-groups/#app-groups).

To see the meters on your own probe, go to **Customize → View Meters**.
