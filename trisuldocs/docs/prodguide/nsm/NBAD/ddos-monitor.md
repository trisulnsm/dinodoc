# DDoS Monitor

**DDoS Monitor** watches for volumetric attacks — the kind of traffic spikes and floods that try to overwhelm a host or link outright rather than sneak past it quietly. Use it to catch a DDoS attempt early and see which hosts or links are under pressure.
