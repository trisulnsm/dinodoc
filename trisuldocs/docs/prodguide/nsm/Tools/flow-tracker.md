# Flow Tracker

:::info Navigation
:point_right: Go to **Tools &rarr; Flow Tracker**
:::

Flow Trackers let you quickly find and track top network flows that match specific criteria, such as high bandwidth usage, long-lived connections, traffic entering or leaving your network, or other flow characteristics.

Use Flow Trackers when you need to identify significant or specific flows across large volumes of flow data. You can use Trisul's built-in trackers or create your own tracking criteria.

For detailed information on viewing, managing, and creating Flow Trackers, see [Flow Trackers](/docs/guide/ug/flow/tracker).
